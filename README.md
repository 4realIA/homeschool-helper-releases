# Sparrow Schoolhouse

Downloads for Sparrow Schoolhouse, friendly K–7 practice in math, reading, Bible and history for Christian homeschool families.

**[Get the latest version →](https://github.com/4realIA/homeschool-helper-releases/releases/latest)**

- **Mac:** download the `.dmg`, open it and drag Sparrow Schoolhouse into Applications.
- **Windows:** download and run the `setup.exe`. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

Once it's installed, the app checks here for new versions. A parent can install them from 🔒 Parent → App updates.

## Why a sparrow?

> Are not two sparrows sold for a farthing? and one of them shall not fall on the ground without your Father. But the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Fear ye not therefore, ye are of more value than many sparrows.
>
> — Matthew 10:29–31 (KJV)

In Jesus's day, sparrows were the cheapest birds in the market: two for a penny. Yet He said that not one of them falls without the Father knowing, and that we are worth more than many sparrows. No child is too small to be noticed by God, and no day's lesson is too small to matter.

The *schoolhouse* is your home: the kitchen table, the couch, wherever your family learns together. Sparrow Schoolhouse is meant to be a small, faithful help there: a few minutes of practice at a time, with every child known by name.
